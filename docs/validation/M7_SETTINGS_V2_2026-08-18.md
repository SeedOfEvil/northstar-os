## What the slice changes

Settings previously rendered three hand-written pages with hardcoded rows.
Every control is now declared into a catalog together with the controller
accessor that actually reads, writes, or performs it, so the surface cannot
drift from the behavior it claims to expose.

Registering an entry whose accessors cannot back its declared kind is refused,
which is what keeps a non-functioning control from reaching the screen.

### A defect the tests caught

`DesktopSettingsTest::findsRepresentativeSettingsBySearch` failed on the first
build: the query `log out` returned `about.launchlog` ahead of `session.end`.
Substring matching found `out` inside `about`, and a multi-word keyword such as
`log out` could never match a single query token. Commit `042c8e6` scores whole
words and splits multi-word keywords into their words. The case passes in the
candidate.

### Method note

`northstar-shell` has no `--qml-self-test` option; passing one starts a full
shell that never returns. The QML load check above was therefore performed with
a bounded offscreen start of the installed binary, and the resulting process
was confirmed gone afterwards. The live session shell was not disturbed by it.

## Honest capability reporting

On this lane the probe finds no mixer, no wireless interface, no Bluetooth
adapter, and no brightness control. Those settings are declared read-only or
unavailable and display the reason their own controller reported, rather than
being rendered as toggles that would silently do nothing. Wi-Fi, Bluetooth,
brightness, and night light have no writable backend in this build at all;
that is a property of the current implementation, not of the VM.

The writable controls this slice actually exposes are dark appearance, Files
grid view, output volume, Do Not Disturb, and the declared actions.

## Pre-existing defects found and deliberately not fixed here

Starting the shell surfaced two defects that already exist on `main` and are
outside this slice. Both were left for separate focused changes:

1. `src/shell/SearchOverlay.qml` binds `lunar.borderStrong` (line 103) and
   `lunar.selection` (line 220); neither exists in `LunarPalette`. The selected
   result in unified search consequently has no highlight.
2. `src/shell/FileBrowserWindow.qml` binds Cut, Copy, Paste, and Undo with
   `sequence:` rather than `sequences:`, leaving their alternate platform key
   sequences dead. This is the same defect fixed for the Text Editor in
   `b8dab58`.
